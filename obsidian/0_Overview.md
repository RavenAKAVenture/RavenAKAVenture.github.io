Attune has become Magic. In addition, mages have a secondary dots table for their spells.

Each school of magic is associated with one or more skill categories. You gain dots in the schools of magic by gaining dots in the associated mundane skills.


**learning spells**

When you take a dot in a normal skill, you can take the same dot in an associated spell school _if_ your resistance roll for that skill category is equal or greater to the dot you’re taking. Being more well rounded generally is necessary to improve as a mage. Your 7 starting dots in character creation contribute to this as normal. Examples:

-_You take a dot in wreck and a dot in Study; your first dot in each. You didn’t have any dots in primal or entropy, so you take a dot in each._

_-You buy your third dot in magic, and you have 1 dot in sway and command. You have 2 dots in Spirit already, so you can take the third._

_-You take your third dot in magic, but your resolve resistance is only 2: You have dots in magic and sway. You can’t increase your Arcane from 2 to 3. But wait! Entropy is also associated with resolve. You have one dot in study, survey, and tinker, so your_ resolve _resistance is 3. You didn’t take the third dot in an insight skill, but that doesn’t matter, because you still took a third dot in an associated skill, and your resistance is high enough—you can increase your entropy from 2 to 3._

Whenever you gain a dot in a magic school, you immediately learn that many levels worth of spells from that school. So, the first dot teaches you 1 level one spell, the second a level 2 spell—or 2 level 1 spells-- and so on. Your dots gained during character creation apply, as before.

If you gain a dot in a normal skill and have no valid magic dot to take, learn that many spell levels of an associated school instead.

**other notes**
Every spell has a level, between 1 and 4, and a school. You need dots in the school equal to the spell’s level to learn it. For example, if you want to learn heal, a level one creation spell, you just need 1 dot in creation. If you want to learn fireball, you’ll need 3 dots in primal.

There are no prepared spells, either you know a spell and can use it or you don’t.

A mage’s maximum mana is the sum of:

3 mana, base,
	+their dots in Magic
and one of:
	+the value of the magic school with the most dots
	OR
	+the total number of magic schools in which you have any dots

Each spell costs mana equal to its level. All mana is restored at the beginning of downtime.

Traits:
-_accurate:_ cast with +1D
-_swift:_ possible to execute when your character might not otherwise be able to act(_as though you had reflexes)_. This speed may mean a roll is not required, though this is by the GM’s discretion.
-_reaction:_ can be cast as part of a resistance roll for +1 mana, or during another player’s resistance roll to improve it.

_Committing_ mana reduces your maximum mana by that much for the rest of the mission. Activating an effect that requires committing mana doesn’t require an action. If an effect sustained by committed mana is dispelled or expires, the committed mana reduction remains.
Committed mana is cleared at the beginning of downtime.
