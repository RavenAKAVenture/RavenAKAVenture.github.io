abilities:
Reflexes: When there’s a question about who acts first, the answer is you.

Scout: When you gather information to locate a target, you get detailed answers to these questions: What signs do I find that show where they’re going? How can I catch up to them, cut them off, or set up an ambush ahead? When you hide in a prepared position or use camouflage, add +1d to any rolls you make.

Infiltrator: You are not affected by quality or Tier when you bypass security measures. This includes guards and patrols.

Daredevil: When you roll a desperate action, you get +1d to your roll if you also take -1d to any resistance rolls against consequences from your action.

Ambush: When you attack from hiding or spring a trap, you get +1d to your roll.
