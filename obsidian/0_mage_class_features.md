Dreamer: You are practiced in the art of using lyrium to project yourself consciously into the fade. Your familiarity allows you to attempt to search out specific spirits or locations, though you may still require certain knowledge or proximity in the physical world to succeed.

Ritualist: You possess the flexibility of mind to devise entirely new spells.

Fade Sense: You’re always aware of supernatural entities in your presence. When you gather information about spirits or the fade, you get detailed answers.

Warded: You may expend your special armor to resist a supernatural consequence, or to push yourself when you contend with or employ arcane forces.

Focus: You don’t suffer reduced effect while casting spells unarmed or under duress.

Sculpt spells: If an ally would suffer consequences from your magic, you can fully mitigate them when you take the protect action.

Specialist: Choose a spell family. Spells of the chosen family cost -1 mana to cast, minimum 1.

  

Items
Fine Staff/Wand
small lyrium vial
	(lyrium is non-trivial to acquire. When you use it, mark a segment on a 4-length clock—when its full, you’ll have to seek out a way to replenish your stock.)
