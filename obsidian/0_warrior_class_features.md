**Abilities**
Battleborn: You may expend your special armor to reduce harm from an attack in combat or to push yourself during a fight.

Not to be trifled With: You can push yourself to do one of the following.

- perform a feat of physical force that verges on the superhuman

- engage a small group on equal footing in close combat.

Vigorous: permanently fill one segment of your healing clock,

Tough as nails: you gain a second space to record level 3 Harm. When you suffer a mortal wound, you may mark 3 Stress to stabilize it and stay alive

Mule: +2 load limits (6 discreet, 8 conspicuous, 9 encumbered, 10 max)
