title: Arcane Spells
---

Level one:

arcane bolt: Cast a seeking, piercing bolt of spirit energy at a single target. _Accurate_

arcane shield: Commit one mana to create an intangible barrier around yourself. Grants +1D to prowess resistance rolls while active.

Level two:

energy barrage: One arcane bolt wasn’t enough. How about 12?

_When you cast this spell, you can choose whether to target a single enemy, or have the bolts seek a large group. It has potency and scale against single targets, and doesn’t suffer penalties from scale against groups up to 12._

_Notably, doesn’t share arcane bolt’s accurate quality._

fade step: You let invisible waves of magic carry you forward, blurring ahead a short distance.

_This isn’t teleportation. You still need to be able to physically reach your destination. As long as you have an unobstructed path, though, you can dash 30~feet in less than a second. Doesn’t need to be a straight line._

Barrier: grant an ablative shield in a small circle. While short lived, it will absorb incoming physical harm before decaying. _Reaction._

_This is effectively a free box of armor to tick when resisting physical harm, reducing it by one level, and can be stacked with regular armor. It disappears after a single use._

Level three:

repulsion field: Waves of force radiate from you, making approach difficult. Creatures larger than people will be less effected.
_Swift. Improves your position in situations with threats involving incoming physical harm._

True seeing: perceive things for what they truly are. Reveals glamours, invisibility, and the nature of complex magical workings.

Level four:

Invigorate: When you or a teammate would take stress from a resistance roll, you can instead commit that much mana.

arcane field: You or your allies may choose one or more of the following effects when casting a spell. Pay one additional mana for each effect.

-the spell gains scale

-the spell gains potency

-the spell is resistant to dispelling/anti-magic effects.
