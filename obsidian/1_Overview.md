Casting magic will still usually be part of an action. Either you’re using a spell to increase your position or effect for an action you could already physically attempt(Anyone can burn a house down, but you can certainly burn it down faster and more spectacularly with a fireball), or the spell is itself the action(“I want to fade step past these goons”). In the first case, you can use the action rating for the intent of what you’re doing(trying to destroy a bridge with earthquake? Sounds achievable with wreck.); in the second just use magic. Spells can be setup actions, but not group actions—_unless_ two or more mages are working together to cast the spell. In that case, choose one to lead the action as usual, and each member of the action spends mana. As with any action in Blades, if there’s truly no risk or dramatic tension in the scene, you can just cast it.

  
Mages in Thedas use focuses. A staff is conventional, but there are other options, like wands and orbs. Spellblades will use their weapons as focuses. A mage can still cast without their focus, but its both more challenging and more dangerous. Mechanically, your position will be riskier.
