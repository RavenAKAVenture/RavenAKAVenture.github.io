Hello gamers.

I might put this hack/module out there at some point, but it isn’t there yet, so please don’t expect it to be overly formal or professional yet. I’m aiming to make it feel similar to the writing of the core blades in the dark book. You can judge for yourself how well I’ve managed that.

As for what to expect:

_The document is incomplete._ I am posting it early, and in incomplete state, by request, and because I agree it will be beneficial for you all to have eyes on it.

I think I have enough put into place to get by, given that Blades is a robust foundation. We’ll be making it up as we go. Feedback and ideas are welcomed; I ask only that we don’t let them interrupt the session. Hold onto the thought until we’re finished, or shoot it to me in dms.

With those qualifiers out of the way.

To guide my work, I went into the table of contents for Blades in the Dark and copied it over, then started writing out my changes in the places were the module differed from the source material. Not every section has been filled out; I’ve left the empty headers in to give myself space to make notes should they come up, but for those sections that remain empty you can assume the fundamental mechanics remain unchanged.

_(Many of the empty sections are also the more metatextual notes and commentary present in Blades. I’d like to add my own thoughts, but those aren’t my priority.)_

The two biggest changes mechanically are the war game—which is based on the foundation of blade’s faction system—and magic, which is the part that’s given me the greatest headache. Sam, I am shaking your hand in solidarity. Good grief.

I think the magic works. At the worst, I can just rule it vibes based, the way attuning works in blades, but I have written down a mostly complete list of spells.

The way spell acquisition works...lets just say I’m not confident in it. Its my first iteration, and I reserve the right to change it.

Next up is a rough pitch for the setting and module, followed by the meat of the rules text.

I recommend you read _Setting-Preamble, Setting_, _The Players,_ and _The Characters_. After that, you should be reader to look through character creation and think about your playbook.
