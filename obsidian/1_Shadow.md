Some rogues are more the swashbuckling type, dashing scoundrels, lords of fortune, eager for glory. Not you. You’re absolutely committed to the unseen path—stealth as your first and last creed. No rogue should fight fair, and that starts with the enemy never knowing you were even there.

Your abilities look a lot like magic. How do you do it? Were you touched by a spirit, like a spirit warrior? Do you have a sensitivity for the fade, a minor talent, not enough to truly cast spells or be considered a full mage, but enough to give you a special edge? Or are they granted by a carefully guarded enchantment or artifact?

  
Starting Dice:
Prowl 2
Finesse 1

Cloak of Shadows: You wrap the fade around yourself, becoming shadowy and insubstantial for a few moments. Take 2 stress when you shift, plus 1 stress for each extra feature: _It lasts a few minutes rather than moments—the veil protects you from spells and projectiles—the veil extends to your party_

Specter: You may expend your special armor to resist a consequence from detection or

security measures, or to push yourself for a feat of athletics or stealth.

Many Faces: When you sway, consort, or finesse a conversation, identifying details will—at your discretion—slip from the other party’s mind until face to face with you again.
	_They’ll remember the conversation happened, but not who it was with._

Fly on the wall: Even the possibility of your hidden presence is a powerful card on the table. You can assist a member of your party without being present, or alternatively assist them without revealing yourself.
	_You can’t use this multiple times in one setting—your foes will get wise, or else decide they have no choice but to act despite the threat of your intervention._
	
Ghostly Passage: You can push yourself to do one of the following: Perform a feat of athletics that verges on the superhuman—Evade harm with a burst of extreme speed and grace.
