Many myths and tall tales across Thedas speak in hushed tones of those with the power to take on different forms. While the tales of witches stealing men’s faces are fanciful in the extreme, shapeshifting is a very real art, known only by a scarce few apostates in the far corners of Thedas. It is, notably, a discipline passed down by certain Dalish Keepers.

  
You can’t cast spells while you’re shifted.

  
Shpaeshifting is not a discipline taught in the circles—who was your mentor, and why did they teach you such a rare art?

  
Starting actions:
Magic 1
Prowl 1
Hunt 1

  
Change form: Commit 2 mana to shift into a form you know. Pay 1 mana to shift to another form, or temporarily back to human shape.

Hunter’s Instincts: You get +1D to discover or corner your targets while shifted, and you can expend your special armor to resist a consequence of surprise or mental harm(fear, confusion, losing track of someone).

Beast Tongue: Through your magic, you can make yourself understood to people while shifted.

Tough Hide: You can tick your armor boxes while shifted(regardless of form), and they don’t count toward your load.

Herbalist: When you invent or craft a creation with alchemical features, you get +1 result level to

your roll (a 1-3 becomes a 4/5, etc.). You begin with one special formula already known.
