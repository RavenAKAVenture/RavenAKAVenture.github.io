You’re not possessed, but some people wouldn’t be able to tell the difference. Your mind was, at some point, touched by a spirit—likely benevolent, but maybe less so. Mages bring the fade through into the world; though not a mage, you do have a little piece of the fade in you, and the end result is abilities that look a lot like magic.

Work with the GM to choose the nature of the spirit that granted you these powers. Choose two schools of magic that align with that spirit’s nature.

Did this encounter come about by accident, or did you seek it out? Do you consider this a tainted blessing, a higher calling, or something else?

You have an innate immunity to possession and can take the Fade Sense mage ability.

Starting actions:
Skirmish: 1
Magic: 1
Consort: 1

Blessed Blade: Your weapons are honed with the edge of raw creation. You have potency against supernatural enemies, and conventional heavy armor or hides don’t hinder your attacks.

Veiled weave: You can expend your special armor to push yourself when combating demons, abominations, or mages, or to resist a consequence caused by arcane forces.

Waking influence: You can push yourself to replicate the effect of a level one spell aligned with your spirit’s nature, and you gain +1 die on rolls to resist spells from those schools of magic.

Language of Creation: Your understanding of the nature of spirits lets you communicate with any demon, no matter how twisted its nature. You get +1D to consort with them.

Supernatural Endurance: You get +1 stress box and are immune to most mundane poisons, intoxicants, hallucinogens, and so on.
