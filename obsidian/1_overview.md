Instead of scores, you’ll have _missions._ Missions begin the same way a score would; with Engagement. Choose a plan, as usual, and a detail if possible.

Each mission has not just a plan but an objective. If successful, you’ll advance the rebellion’s goals.
	Sabotage: hinder your foe’s plans.
		_If you’re clever, you can double dip. For example: stealing funds that were going to soldier’s wages_
	Espionage: perform counter-intelligence to misdirect your enemies. While the reconnaissance downtime action has been performed and you can see either army’s current objectives, you may attempt to change one of those objectives
		Acquisition: Gain an ally, item, or other advantage to strengthen your position.
		_If you’re in need of funds, this can function like a more traditional score, granting gold._
	-Balance: Tip the scales by giving one faction an edge over the other.
		_Sabotage will set one faction back, and while this might create an opening for another, its not explicitly pushing them towards it. Balance advances a clock or plan for one faction at the expense of another._
	Mercy: Unlike a crew of scoundrels, you’re part of a movement that wants to help people. In the midst of a civil war, innocents are going to get hurt. Helping them won’t necessarily grant you immediate material advantage, but it will build support for your movement.
	Scramble: Something bad is about to go down, and you need to handle it without time to prepare. These can happen when certain enemy clocks fill; a last minute chance to put a wrench in the works.

Your missions will usually begin from the chamber at the center of the eluvian network, cutting to where you exit the crossroads, and end when you return through the mirror. However, sometimes you’ll be sent to a city with several missions, in which case you’ll operate out of the city and prepare your plans for engagement more traditionally.
_A mission chain in a city will have limited or no downtime while being completed._

Sometimes you’ll be sent to react to a situation you didn’t see coming. If a mission is sending you into an unknown situation without time to prepare, simply choose your plan and roll 1D for pure luck.
