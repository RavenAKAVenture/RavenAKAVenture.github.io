The threat of making a mess, when you can vanish and reappear halfway across the continent in hours, is not to you as individuals, but rather to the resistance as a whole. Overplay your hand, make too much noise, and your enemies will take note and retaliate—perhaps against the very people you were trying to help.

Heat remains. Instead of entanglements, enemy factions will make _advancements._ Instead of wanted levels, the movement will take _setbacks._ These are really bad. Taking a fifth setback means total defeat, and they’re harder to undo than wanted levels.

**Payoff**
2 rep: minor victory
4 rep: a small victory, noticeable advance
6 rep: a meaningful victory
8 rep: A significant victory; a decisive move
10+rep: A major victory; turning the tide
_subtract rep if elven civilians were harmed in the crossfire._
You can spend your rep as though it were gold.

**Heat**

0 heat: Smooth & quiet; low exposure.
2 heat: Contained; standard exposure.
4 heat: Loud & chaotic; high exposure.
6 heat: Wild; devastating exposure.
+1 for a high profile target
+2 if witnesses saw you killing
	if death was involved in the score, increase veil instability in the region by 1.
+1 if the operation was in a city with higher security/infamy than influence
Halve all heat after calculation.
	If witnesses saw you using magic, begin a clock. You might end up with Templars working more closely with your primary enemies.

**Advancements**
You’re not just concerned about personal reprisals for your cell, though those can happen—you’re worried about the balance of the civil war shifting.
Heat 0-3: new assets(1-3), momentum(4-5), ripples in the water(6)
Heat 4-5: Retribution(4-5), Restless Spirits(6)
Heat 6+: Salting the Earth(1-3), Decisive Victory(4-5), Flipped(6)

momentum: whichever faction has the advantage in the war makes further advances. If the conflict is balanced in a stalemate, a fortune roll determines which side gains an advantage

new assets: One side increases its treasury or finishes mustering troops.

ripples in the water: the veil weakens in a region.

decisive victory: The side with an advantage succeeds in a battle or siege, tipping the balance of power.

Retribution: The powers that be take punitive action against the people you’re trying to free.

Restless Spirits: a demon makes an attempt on a mage in the party, or among their allies.
