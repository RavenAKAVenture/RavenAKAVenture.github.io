Any good rogue arms themselves with stealth, but for assassins, it’s just another tool in their arsenal; One implement among many, each well oiled tool carefully chosen for the purpose of delivering murder as efficiently as possible.


Did you receive professional training? Do you take any pleasure in your work, or is it purely a means to an end? Is your technique flashy? Controlled? Brutal and efficient?

  
Starting actions:
Hunt 2
Prowl 1

Cloak and Dagger: When you use a disguise or other form of covert misdirection, you get +1d to rolls to confuse or deflect suspicion. When you throw off your disguise, the resulting surprise gives you the initiative in the situation.

Exploit Weakness: You get +1D against a target whose secrets you know.

Single-Minded: You can expend your special armor to resist a consequence that would obstruct you from reaching your target, or to push yourself to overcome bodyguards or other security.

Hidden Blades: while only carrying light load, you can conceal just about anything on your person.

Relentless: Killing a target refreshes your special armor.
