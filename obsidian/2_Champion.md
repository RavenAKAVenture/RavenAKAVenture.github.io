You’re a leader. Not just a natural one, but a proven one as well. You’ve commanded people before, and earned their respect for your ability to control the fight.

Did you fight for a cause? Or did you simply rise to the challenge in a crisis?

Starting actions:
Skirmish 1
Survey 1
Command 1

Line in the Sand: They can’t get past you. At your discretion, you can take on any harm that would be suffered by an ally in arm’s length instead, and can cover allies for physical consequences/harm that a single individual might not normally be able to.

Leader: When you Command a cohort in combat, they continue to fight when they would otherwise break (they’re not taken out when they suffer level 3 harm). They gain +1 effect and 1 armor.

Battlefield Instinct: Twice per mission, describe how your situational awareness or leadership skills add +1d to a teammate’s roll.

Expertise: When you lead a group action, you can suffer only 1 stress at most, regardless of the number of failed rolls.

Adamant: You have extensive conditioning with your armor. Armor and heavy armor cost 1 load each.
