**Race**
You can play a human, elf, dwarf, or Qunari. Dwarves are the only mechanically distinct race: They cannot be mages*, and get +1D to resistance rolls against magic effects.

The major distinguishing point between humans is their country of origin. Each other race can also be found all over Thedas, but also have further distinct cultural splits, so they’ll choose a lineage as well. When choosing a heritage, keep in mind that many of these places are very far from Orlais, where the game will be centered. You won’t be stopped from choosing somewhere in northern Thedas, but be prepared to explain how you ended up here.

Your playbook begins with three action dots already placed. You get to add four more dots (for seven total). At the start of the game, no action rating may have more than two dots (unless a special ability tells you otherwise). Assign your four dots like this:
	
 Put one dot in any action that you feel reflects your character’s heritage.
 Put one dot in any action that you feel reflects your character’s background.
Assign two more dots anywhere you please (the max rating remains two, for now).
	_Dwaves can’t allocate any dots in Magic._

Choose a special ability.
	_Blades vets: Veteran advances are not available by default. They can be acquired via a crew ability._

	All playbooks have the following xp triggers:  
-you embodied a core nature of your playbook.
-you expressed your beliefs, heritage, or background.
-you sacrificed for others or the cause.
	Desperate actions grant a crew XP instead of personal XP. This can only happen twice per score.
