In Origins, the ‘elemental’ spells here are split into their own trees. So, you’d buy flame blast and cone of cold individually. Here, having elemental blast means you can cast flame blast, cone of cold, and shock at your discretion. Simply declare what element you’re using from among fire, ice, and lightning whenever using any of the elemental spells. (Earth is still its own thing, because it doesn’t quite follow the same pattern as the others.)

  

Fire has potency against most living things. Use fire when you want to deal raw damage—if you’re willing to risk the collateral damage too.

Ice is great for crowd control. You’ll often improve your position by impairing your enemies.

Lightning is fast and great for interrupting opposing mages, or anyone trying to carry out a maneuver that requires concentration.

  

Level one:

rock armor: You make your skin hard as stone. When resisting physical harm, you may pay one mana instead of ticking armor; reduce the harm as usual.

elemental blast: Unleash the elements in front of you in a cone.

_Legally distinct burning hands._

Level two:

stone fist: Hurl a small boulder at high speeds. This will knock many opponents over, interrupting and slowing them down for a beat in combat, and is also rather destructive to environmental objects less sturdy than a very thick wall.

elemental weapons: commit two mana. enchant the party’s weapons with an element, granting potency to attacks with those weapons against anyone vulnerable to the chosen element.

(i_): Most humanoids are vulnerable to fire._

elemental mines: inscribe a rune on the ground that will explode in a burst of the chosen element when walked over. Make the Magic roll when the rune detonates, not when you inscribe it. Takes about five seconds to prime.

Level three:

storm cage: surround a target with crackling lightning. It suffers a painful shock if it tries to leave, and most creatures won’t have the persistence to force their way through.

chain lightning: Unleash an arcing lance of lightning. It always has scale.

elemental wall: Summon a 20~foot long and 10 foot high barrier of ice, fire, or lightning. The ice is a physical barrier, and lasts longer than the others; Fire and lightning can be walked through, even if most reasonable opponents won’t try.

fireball: Take a wild guess.

Level four:

petrify: Attempt to turn a target into stone. Effective on creatures humanoid or smaller in size, but can also be used on inanimate solid objects(ex: fusing a door into a wall). When used on living creatures, the resulting stone is brittle and easily shattered. The effect will not expire naturally, but can be reversed via magic.

_Templars, mages, demons, and the like may possess resistance to this effect._

elemental storm: Summon a blizzard, lightning storm, or blazing inferno in a large radius(~20ft) at a point of your choosing. When cast in enclosed spaces, the forces will be condensed and increased in intensity.

earthquake: Cause a violent quake in a large radius around a point of your choosing. People struggle to stand, and buildings may suffer structural damage or collapse. The quake continues until the end of the scene—you can pay its mana cost again to end it. It can also be ended via a dispel magic effect.
