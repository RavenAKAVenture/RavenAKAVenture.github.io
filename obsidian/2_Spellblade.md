The very first mages to wield weapon and spellcraft were the ancient elven arcane warriors, who called the discipline _Dirth'ena Enasalin._ Before the fall of the Dales, there were those among the Emerald Knights who kept the art alive. In the present day, the closest analogue are the Chantry sanctioned Knight Enchanters, whose techniques, will distinct, are still based on those ancient practitioners.

  
You’ve trained your body as well as your mind, and you weave an elegant dance of steel and magic. What drew you to this technique? Were you given formal teaching, or did you simply pick up a weapon yourself? Do you have any connection to the history of the art?

Starting Actions:
Magic 2
Finesse 1


Combat magic: You can turn your magic inward, surpassing human limits. Commit 2 mana. While combat magic is active, you may use magic for any prowess roll—commit an additional mana for each time you do this beyond the first-- and you can spend 1 mana for each of the following effects you’d like to add to an action.
	-increase your effective scale in combat
	-you can move with superhuman speed and agility
	-create a shimmering shield that grants you +1 to all resistance rolls

Battlemage: you can push yourself to do one of the following: Infuse your weapon for a powerful magically enhanced strike, or use your blade to magically reflect attacks.

Combat Clarity: The trials you endure only fuel your focus. Whenever you take stress from resisting, gain half that much mana(rounded down, minimum 1).

Fade Shroud: The best defense is a good offense. You can treat your conventional armor and heavy armor boxes as special armor.

Spells

	_Level 1, Creation._
	_Works on just about anything short of a sturdy castle wall._

Disruption Field: fill a dome-shaped area with magical energy that slows and weakens your enemies.
	_Level 2, Arcane._
	_Enemies larger than the field are immune._

Shimmering Shield: you can pay 2 mana when suffering violent consequences to negate them, rather than reduce them.
	_Level 3, Spirit._

Haste: You Commit 3 mana for +1D to prowess rolls, at the cost of -1D to resist consequences on those rolls. You can act on equal footing as anyone with _reflexes.
