**The Fade**
All conflict, but especially mages and blood magic, weakens the veil and draws the attention of demons. This isn’t something easily repaired: repeated scarring will permanently weaken the veil in a region, making doing magic there riskier.

**Spirits and Demons**


**Possession**
Possession is a constant risk for mages. This risk is represented as a new, exciting range of possible consequences. Any time you roll magic, you expose yourself to the attentions of the denizens of the fade. At a risky position, this may merely mean the beginning of a clock, but at desperate you risk a demon appearing wholesale.
	_(Immediate, permanent loss of control of your character is Bad Gameplay, so we’re opting for a little ludonarrative dissonance. If full possession is on the table, treat it with the same mechanical weight as fatal harm.)_
Possession can take on varied forms. When forced or initiated unwillingly, the result is the mage becoming an abomination, their self obliterated as their body undergoes grotesque, twisted changes.

When willingly entered into by both participants, the results are much less destructive. The mage can gain significant strength from the spirit, though the exact form this takes can vary. In this situation, the greatest risk to the equilibrium of the pair is the host acting in ways contrary to the spirit’s nature. If this becomes a pattern, it can cause the spirit to surface in unpredictable and violent ways.

Possession can be undone in some cases, but doing so requires special knowledge and either lyrium, blood magic, or a powerful dreamer. It’s not a common undertaking.
