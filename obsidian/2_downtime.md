Downtime Activities
Indulge Vice - Acquire Asset – Long-Term Project – Recover – Train – Reconnaissance – Side Jobs

Indulge Vice
	pay 1 coin to clear stress.
**Train**
	max once. add 1 xp to an xp wheel.

**Reconnaissance**
	Choose a city/region and roll as though you were gathering information. Depending on your success on this fortune roll, you’ll reveal plans by opposing factions in the area: What their projects are, and how much progress they’ve made. Their activities will stay visible to you for the rest of the month.

**Side Jobs**
Make a fortune roll based on the type of work you choose to do, gaining 1-3 coin based on the result.
