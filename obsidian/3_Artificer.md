Knives, bows, stealth, that’s all well and good, but you hate getting your hands dirty, and there are so many wonderful toys in the world for someone with the right know-how or connections. You have both.

  
An artificer’s training is rare. How did you learn? Did you steal the materials and experiment, earning the knowledge through hard work, and maybe a missing finger or two? Were you trained by a dwarf who needed an apprentice, or some other eccentric recluse willing to share their genius?
	_Like the leech, artificers get a bandolier, letting them bring loads of useful tools to encounters. If their abilities seem underwhelming, that’s why—lots of their power is loaded in their equipment._

  
Starting Actions
Tinker 2
Study 1

  
Sharpshooter: You can push yourself to do one of the following:

-make a ranged attack at extreme distance beyond what’s normal for the weapon

-unleash a barrage of rapid fire to suppress the enemy.

Alchemist: When you invent or craft a creation with alchemical features, you get +1 result level to your roll (a 1-3 becomes a 4/5, etc.). You begin with one special formula already known.

Saboteur: When you Wreck, your work is much quieter than it should be and the damage is very well-hidden from casual inspection.
