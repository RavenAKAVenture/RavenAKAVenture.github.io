Level one:

heal: A single target immediately makes a healing roll with a bonus die. This can target yourself.

heroic offense: you empower a single ally. Choose one: They gain potency for a single action in combat, or they aren’t affected by your enemy’s level or quality for the rest of the scene.

glyph of paralysis: Inscribe a glyph on the ground; it fades into invisibility. The first person or animal to cross it is paralyzed.

_Creatures larger than the glyph will be held for mere seconds._

spell wisp: commit one mana to summon a tiny green wisp. The wisp is a level 0 expert that can group into your Magic rolls for spellcasting.

Level two:

rejuvenate: The target clears their special armor box, _or_ clears 1 stress.

heroic aura: empower a single ally. Harm they would suffer from incoming projectiles is reduced by 1 level for the rest of the scene.

glyph of warding: This glyph averts blows, projectiles, and even offers some protection from magic to those standing inside it.

_Mechanically, your position is better when facing these threats._

Grease: Summons a flammable grease slick on the ground.

Level three:

regeneration: a more powerful sustained heal. The target ticks their heal clock by 1 each time they act, for the rest of the scene or until they’ve filled their healing clock twice.

heroic defense: empower an ally. Harm they would suffer from magic is reduced by 1 level for the rest of the scene.

glyph of repulsion: Anyone trying to cross the boundary of this glyph is tossed back with great force.

glyph of concealment: Inscribe a circle on the ground, invisible to any but the caster and those inside the circle when it was created. Any creature standing within the circle is invisible as long as they remain there.

spellbloom: Create a 10ft radius circle of energizing magic. Anyone standing in it reduces the mana cost of all spells they cast by one(minimum one). The bloom lasts one minute.

Level four:

mass rejuvenation: Every member of your party clears 2 stress.

glyph of neutralization: inscribes a glyph on the ground that neutralizes all magic, dispels all effects and prevents spellcasting within its bounds.

_-Buffs and debuffs, friendly or hostile, are dispelled_

_-passive, non-spell magical effects—runes, enchantments, and so on—cease to function while in the radius of this glyph._

_-spells that would cross the boundary of this glyph dissipate._

_-spell effects requiring committed mana are lost; the mana stays committed._

Glyph of Sealing:

Revive: Spend all of your mana. A character that took fatal harm _this scene_ is brought back to life; They’re still incapacitated, with level 3 harm.

Stinging swarm: Commit 4 mana to summon a swarm of biting insects. Treat them as an expert; their level is equal to yours. As a swarm, they aren’t much affected by normal weapons(or slowed by armor), but are vulnerable to fire.
