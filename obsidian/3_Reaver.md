That there’s power in blood is well known; yet, the fact is typically only exploitable by mages. You live for exceptions and transgressions, which is why you drank the blood of a high dragon, stealing its strength.

How did you acquire this blood? Did you truly earn it, seeking out and slaying the beast yourself, or did you get it through other means? Such blood can be found on the black market, if you look hard enough, or granted as a boon by certain groups for great deads.

Starting actions:
Wreck 1
Skirmish 1
Command 1

Devour: You can immediately make a healing roll whenever you slay a living creature.

Aura of Pain: You quite literally share your pain with your enemies, radiating an aura of pain when you skirmish or wreck. Take a level two injury, spirit scars, to gain scale and potency(against living creatures) on your roll, as waves of pain incapacitate lesser enemies.

Blood Frenzy: You can expend your special armor to ignore incapacitation from level three injuries, and you gain +1 die to all action and resistance rolls while you have a level three injury.

Savage: You can take a level two injury instead of paying stress to push yourself, but you take a cumulative -1D to all resistance rolls until the end of the mission when you do so.
