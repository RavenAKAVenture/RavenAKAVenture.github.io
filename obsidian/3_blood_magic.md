title: Blood magic
---

Powerful, forbidden, and risky: Any mage can do it. Some spells are exclusively possible through blood magic, but such arts are not easily found or learned. Bargaining with a demon is the fastest way to unlocking them.

For those that don’t want to go that far, though, a small sacrifice is all you need. Any mage can choose to take an injury equal to the level of the spell they’re casting, in place of spending mana; Not that it _has_ to be their own blood: a willing donor or restrained captive will also work. (Yes, this does mean casting a level 4 spell means dealing lethal harm to someone. Don’t look surprised.)
  
You can also choose to split the difference, if you’re just a little shy of the spell you want to cast—a level 3 spell can be cast by paying 2 mana and taking a level 1 injury, or a level 2 injury and 1 mana. This only applies up to level 3 spells, though—casting a level 4 spell via blood magic will always kill someone.

The sacrifice required for Blood magic leaves harm deeper than simple blood loss. Anyone who takes an injury to fuel blood magic suffers a cumulative -1D to heal rolls until the next mission and immediately clears their healing clock. Lvl 1 harm from blood magic does not automatically clear.

The final notable quality of blood magic is, due to its source being power _not_ drawn from the fade, it bypasses most magic-inhibiting effects.
