The art of animating the dead via placing a spirit in the body of the deceased. Taboo and largely misunderstood outside of Nevarra, much of its stigma comes not only from more generalized fear, but clashing cultural beliefs about spirits, the soul, and the afterlife. Despite this, something of a double standard exists, and certain aspects of spirit magic are taught within circles outside Nevarra while carefully avoiding the label “necromancy.”

Were you taught inside Nevarra, or by a Mortalitasi in a foreign land? Is your practice informed by your spiritual beliefs, or is the reverse true?
