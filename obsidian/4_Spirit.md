title: Spirit spells

Level one:

spell shield: commit two mana. While active, you completely negate arcane consequences against you instead of reducing them when resisting, and add +1 die when making the resistance roll.

mana drain: spend 2 stress to drain 2 mana from a target you can see that has it. This can be done as part of casting a higher level spell.

_Mages, demons, and abominations all have mana._

_This isn’t templated to be pushing yourself because that would make the roll more confusing._

walking bomb: conjure a corrosive spiritual poison in a target’s blood. This poison is fast acting and inflicts some immediate harm; however, should the target die in the next 30 seconds, they will explode with incredible force, severely harming anyone in a large radius around the unfortunate soul.

mind blast: Toss everyone around you away with a burst of telekinetic force.

Level two:

dispel magic: remove all magical effects from a single target, _or_, weaken a demon’s hold on the physical world and attempt to push it back into the fade.

_Permanent enchantments are temporarily disabled, not destroyed._

_Effects created by committed mana are dispelled; the mana is not refunded._

_When cast on demons, treat it as an attack with potency. Doesn’t work on abominations._

mana cleanse: choose an amount of mana to pay to cast this spell. That much mana is drained from every spellcaster in a 20 foot radius sphere(you can place this sphere at range).

force field: Suspend a target in a cylinder of impenetrable force. This field lasts for a minute; the target can take no actions, but cannot be harmed either.

Level three:

anti-magic ward: ward a single ally against all other magic for the duration of the scene.

virulent walking bomb: as walking bomb, only far more pernicious. The spray of blood from the detonation will infect those it hits, making chain reactions possible.

telekinetic weapons: commit 2 mana to enchant your party’s melee weapons; this enchantment grants potency against armored foes.

spell might: commit 1 mana to gain +1D to magic rolls to cast spells, in exchange for every spell costing +1 mana.

Level four:

anti-magic burst: as dispel magic, in a large targeted area of effect.

crushing prison: encloses a target in a collapsing cage of telekinetic force, inflicting severe harm.

_Larger than humanoid targets will be held only momentarily._

_Weaker foes have no chance of escaping, and will be crushed to death without intervention._

_Stronger foes may make fortune rolls to break free._

mana clash: as mana cleanse, but those affected also take harm based on the mana drained. If the target had mana committed to a passive effect, the effect is dispelled, and they take additional damage.

_Enemies don’t have injuries the same way players do, but for a rough heuristic, assume 1 level of injury/1 tick per 3 mana lost._

_Damage dealt is based on how much mana the targets lost, not their maximum mana._
