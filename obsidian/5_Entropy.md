title: Entropy spells
---

Unless otherwise stated, entropy debuffs last a minute; you can commit one mana to maintain the spell as long as you have line of sight to the target.

  

A non-exhaustive list of creatures immune to the mental influence of disorient, nightmare, sleep, and waking nightmare:

-golems

-demons

-the undead

  

Level one:

weakness: curse a single target with supernatural enervation. The target has reduced effect in any physical actions they take.

_This will often take the form of improved position when engaging a target. Outside of direct combat, consider what they’re trying to do. They’ll be slower and weaker than usual._

Vulnerability hex: This hex strips away the elemental protections of the target.

_Example: Fire will usually have limited effect against a drake; Because of the drake’s resistance, the fire has no potency,_ and _it’s effect would be reduced. Cast vulnerability, and suddenly a fireball would have great effect._

Disorient: Fog the target’s mind, making it difficult for them to process their surroundings. _Accurate._

drain life: Absorb the vital force of a single target to heal yourself. This spell does limited damage, but immediately lets you make a healing roll.

Level two:

paralyze: What it says on the tin. Single target.

Affliction hex: As vulnerability hex; while still technically single target, this improved hex now affects everyone around the target too.

Horror: force a single target to cower in fear, unable to act.

_Damage doesn’t break the fear._

Level three:

miasma: commit three mana to radiate the weakness spell as a 10ft radius sphere around yourself. Roll Magic each time an enemy enters this radius(this is a fortune roll, without consequences). Partial success hinders their movement; full success hinders them severely enough to impact their ability to attack or defend themselves.

Misdirection hex: Try as they might, the target simply cannot hit what they aim for.

_Mixed successes in combat against a hexed target might still incur other consequences, but cannot inflict harm on the players._

Sleep: put every target in an area to sleep.

_Sleeping targets are completely vulnerable to other mental effects._

_Supernatural creatures are almost universally immune; demons especially._

_Any damage will wake the subjects._

curse of mortality: curse a single target with the inevitability of true death. They cannot be healed by any means, and suffer continuous injury.

_In the absence of a mage who can counter it, this curse is a death sentence to most mortals. It is also a_ slow _death sentence—it won’t kill someone fast enough to be noticeable in combat._

_Larger than humanoid targets might eventually be crippled, but will outlast the curse._

  

Level four:

mass paralysis: paralyze everyone in a large, targeted radius.

death hex: Cripple the target with undiluted suffering. They effectively lose all level/tier.

death cloud: Summon a sinister miasma in a large radius. It lingers for the duration of the scene. Anyone who enters suffers every targeted entropy spell you know.
